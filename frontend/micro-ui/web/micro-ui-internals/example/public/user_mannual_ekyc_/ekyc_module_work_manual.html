Step 1: Locating eKYC Admin Module on Dashboard

Accessing module cards under Core Services based on your assigned staff role.

1After successful login, your browser displays the Employee Home Landing Page showing your greeting, designation (e.g. DDO - Drawing and Disbursing Officer), and active financial year.
2Scroll down to the Core Services card section on your dashboard.
3Locate the eKYC Admin module card. The card displays live summary metrics such as TOTAL EKYC: 3498162.
4Click on the Details button at the bottom-right of the eKYC Admin card to view available sub-modules.
Role Visibility: Module cards displayed under Core Services automatically adapt based on your Keycloak user roles (e.g. EMPLOYEE, EKYC_SURVEYOR, EKYC_SUPERVISOR, EKYC_VENDOR, EKYC_ZRO).

Step 2: Navigating eKYC Admin Sub-Modules

Selecting operational options based on your departmental responsibilities.

1Clicking Details redirects you to the eKYC Admin options page (/digit-ui/employee/module/details).
2Depending on your assigned roles, you will see up to three core operational cards:
• 📥 Inbox (Shows live counter, e.g., 3498162): Click to search, review, and process consumer applications.
• 📊 Admin Dashboard: Click to monitor total eKYC applications, platform performance, and vendor efficiency.
• 💰 M.F DOR FINANCE VIEW: Designed for executive officers to review revenue impact, agency performance, and zone-wise progress.
3Click on any card option arrow (›) to open that module workspace.
Quick Tip: You can also use the left sidebar menu under ALL MODULES → eKYC Admin to jump between modules at any time!

Step 3: eKYC Application Inbox & Searching by K. Number

Retrieving consumer eKYC applications and reviewing submission records.

1Click on Inbox from the eKYC Admin details page (/digit-ui/employee/ekyc/inbox).
2Search by K. Number: Type the consumer's K. Number into the search box at the top and click the blue Search button. Click Clear Search to reset filters.
3Total Records Counter: Displays total applications in the system (e.g. 3498162 TOTAL RECORDS).
4Data Table Columns:
• Application Number: Clickable ID link (e.g., 3354985034, 9881841000).
• Name: Consumer full name (e.g., Meenu Jha, Virender Virender).
• Status: Connection state (ACTIVE / STOPPED).
• Submitted At: Date of application submission.
• Assigned At: Current staff/surveyor assigned.
• eKYC Status: Verification stage tag (SUBMITTED, PENDING, APPROVED).
5Click on any blue Application Number / K. Number link to open full application details, meter photos, and take verification actions.
Action Required for Field Staff: Click on assigned application numbers to perform field surveys, capture meter photos, or approve/reject eKYC data.

• Submission Health: Platform efficiency rate %, average processing latency (seconds/minutes), and system error rate %.

Step 5: M.F DOR FINANCE VIEW (Executive Financial & Zone Analytics)

Macro-level oversight for higher authorities (ZRO, DOR, Finance Officers, and Dept Heads).

Revenue Data & Module Progress

Full financial analytics detailing eKYC coverage, revenue integrity, bill accuracy improvements, and portal adoption across all Delhi Jal Board zones.

Agency Performance Snapshot

Search, filter, and navigate into detailed operational analytics for every assigned vendor agency, evaluating field surveyor productivity and completion rates.

Jurisdiction Performance

Top zones and divisions evaluated from the full agency dataset, highlighting top-performing revenue circles and areas needing intervention.

Critical Alerts & Efficiency

Automated flagging of SLA delays, field verification bottlenecks, workforce activity tracking, and turnaround efficiency across all revenue divisions.

Executive Access: The M.F DOR Finance View is specifically enabled for ZRO (Zone Revenue Officers), DOR, and senior executive officers to ensure complete transparency and data-driven decision making.
