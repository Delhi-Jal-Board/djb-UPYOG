User Manual Guide

Supervisor Registration & Profile Creation Guide

What this page helps with: This manual guides Vendor Agencies step-by-step through creating and mapping Supervisors under their agency account. It covers accessing eKYC Vendor Management from the Vendor Dashboard, opening the Add Supervisor form, entering supervisor profile & contact details, and reviewing mapped supervisors.

Vendor Authority Requirement

Supervisors are created directly by Vendors within the Vendor Management portal. Ensure you have logged in using your registered vendor credentials before proceeding.

How Vendor Logins →

Step 1: Accessing Vendor Dashboard & Clicking eKYC

Logging into the vendor portal workspace and navigating to the eKYC module card.

1 Log into the official Delhi Jal Board Citizen/Vendor Portal using your registered vendor credentials.
2 Upon successful authentication, you will land on the Vendor Portal Dashboard screen featuring services such as My City, Water Tanker, eKYC, Water & Sewerage, and Vendor Management.
3 Locate and click on the eKYC button tile shown on the dashboard.
https://uat-ejalsewa.delhi.gov.in/digit-ui/citizen

Step 2: Navigating to Vendor Management from eKYC

Selecting the Vendor Management action from the Citizen Services eKYC workspace.

1 After clicking the eKYC tile, you will be taken to the eKYC Module Landing Page displaying the banner header.
2 Under the CITIZEN SERVICES > eKYC card container, you will find options: Inbox, Vendor Dashboard, and Vendor Management.
3 Click on the Vendor Management button to open your agency's service search and supervisor configuration page.
https://uat-ejalsewa.delhi.gov.in/digit-ui/citizen/ekyc-home

Step 3: Accessing Vendor Service Search Screen

Viewing vendor details and locating the top-right orange Add button.

1 The Vendor Service Search page opens, displaying tabs for Vendor, Supervisor, and Surveyor.
2 Your vendor details (Vendor Name, Mobile Number, Creation Date, Service Type, Enable/Disable status, and Action buttons) will be visible in the primary data table.
3 Look at the top-right corner of the workspace card to locate the orange Add + button.
https://uat-ejalsewa.delhi.gov.in/digit-ui/citizen/ekyc/vendor-search

Step 4: Selecting Supervisor from Add Menu

Triggering the action dropdown and choosing the Supervisor option.

1 Click on the orange Add + button in the top-right corner of the card.
2 A pop-over menu will expand displaying the option for Supervisor.
3 Click on Supervisor to navigate to the supervisor creation registration form.
https://uat-ejalsewa.delhi.gov.in/digit-ui/citizen/ekyc/vendor-search#add-menu

Step 5: Filling the New Supervisor Registration Form

Entering supervisor personal details, contact information, and operational zone assignment.

Mandatory Information Notice: All fields marked with a red asterisk (*) are required for supervisor user creation and authentication pairing.
1 In the Full Name * field, type the complete legal name of the supervisor.
2 In the Mobile Number * field, enter the 10-digit mobile number (prefixed with +91) which will serve as the supervisor's primary login username.
3 In the Email ID * field, provide a valid email address for receiving system communications.
4 Select the supervisor's Gender * from the dropdown menu (Male / Female / Other).
5 In the Date of Birth * field, select or enter the date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
6 In the Correspondence Address * field, enter the complete physical/residential address of the supervisor.
7 Select the assigned DJB operational Zone * from the dropdown list.
8 In the Description field, optionally enter any relevant supervisor notes or agency comments.
9 Kindly review all entered details thoroughly, then click the blue Submit button in the bottom right corner.
https://uat-ejalsewa.delhi.gov.in/digit-ui/citizen/ekyc/new-supervisor

Step 6: Creation Confirmation & Mapped Supervisor List

Verifying successful supervisor registration and viewing the mapped supervisor records.

SUCCESS CONFIRMATION:
Upon clicking Submit, a green pop-up toast message will display "Supervisor added successfully", confirming account creation.
1 After submission, you will be redirected back to the Vendor Service Search page.
2 Click on the Supervisor tab adjacent to the Vendor tab.
3 Here, vendors can view all their mapped supervisors, search by supervisor name or mobile number, and monitor active operational status.
https://uat-ejalsewa.delhi.gov.in/digit-ui/citizen/ekyc/vendor-search#supervisor-list
