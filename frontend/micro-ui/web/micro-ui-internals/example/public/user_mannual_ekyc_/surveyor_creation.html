Field Surveyor Registration & Mapping Guide

What this page helps with: This manual guides Supervisors through registering and mapping operational Field Surveyors. It details navigating from the Supervisor Dashboard to Vendor Management, completing the New Surveyor registration form with mandatory zone & contact details, and managing mapped surveyors in the search list.

Supervisor Privilege Required

Surveyor creation is an exclusive privilege of registered Supervisors. Supervisors must log in using their credentials to access Vendor Management and register field surveyors.

Supervisor Login Guide →

Step 1: Accessing eKYC Tile from Supervisor Dashboard

Navigating from the main Supervisor portal landing page to the eKYC module workspace.

1 Log into the portal using your Supervisor credentials. You will land on the Supervisor Services Dashboard (showing greeting banner e.g. Good Afternoon! Ekyc Supervisor Mannual).
2 Locate and click on the eKYC button card displayed on the service modules grid.

Step 2: Selecting Vendor Management Card

Opening the eKYC Module workspace options and selecting Vendor Management.

1 After clicking eKYC, the eKYC Module landing page will display available management options under Citizen Services.
2 Click on the Vendor Management option card to proceed to surveyor and supervisor administration.

Step 3: Vendor Service Search & Add Option

Viewing the Surveyor search tab and locating the Add button.

1 You will be redirected to the Vendor Service Search page, where the Surveyor tab is selected by default.
2 Locate the orange Add + button positioned at the top-right corner of the tab container.

Step 4: Selecting Surveyor from Add Dropdown

Opening the action menu to launch the new surveyor registration form.

1 Click on the orange Add + button in the top-right corner.
2 A dropdown menu will appear containing the Surveyor option.
3 Click on Surveyor to open the creation form.

Step 5: Filling Surveyor Basic Details Form

Entering mandatory surveyor details and submitting for profile creation.

Mandatory Form Notice: Ensure all fields marked with a red asterisk (*) are filled accurately before clicking Submit.
1 The New Surveyor registration form (`Surveyor Basic Details`) will load on screen.
2 Fill in all mandatory personal & operational details:
• Full Name *: Enter the surveyor's full legal name.
• Mobile Number *: Enter the 10-digit mobile number (`+91`).
• Email ID *: Enter the surveyor's active email address.
• Gender: Select gender from dropdown menu.
• Date of Birth *: Select or type date in `DD/MM/YYYY` format.
• Correspondence Address *: Enter residential / official postal address.
• Zone *: Select assigned operational zone (e.g. `MAYUR VIHAR`).
• Description: Enter optional operational notes or role details.
3 Carefully review all entered details for accuracy.
4 Click the blue Submit button at the bottom-right corner to save and register the new surveyor.

Step 6: Confirmation & Mapped Surveyor List View

Verifying creation success toast message and reviewing all mapped surveyors.

Surveyor Created Successfully! A green notification box saying "Surveyor added successfully" will pop up at the bottom of the screen upon submission.
1 After clicking Submit, the system saves the record and displays a green confirmation pop-up message: `✔ Surveyor added successfully`.
2 You will be automatically redirected to the Vendor Service Search list screen under the Surveyor tab.
3 Here, Supervisors can view all surveyors mapped to their account, detailing:
• Surveyor Name (e.g. Ekyc Surveyor Mannual)
• Mobile Number (e.g. 7009959137)
• Supervisor Name (e.g. Ekyc Supervisor Mannual)
• Creation Date (e.g. 14/08/2026)
• Enable/Disable toggle status switch.
4 Supervisors can filter mapped surveyors using the Search Name or Mobile Number fields, or export records using the Download CSV button.
