User Manual Guide

Vendor Registration & Onboarding Guide

What this page helps with: This manual helps authorized DJB Employees register and onboard new third-party Vendor agencies into the system. It details navigating to Vendor Management, initiating New Vendor Registration, filling required service & property address forms, submitting profile details, and searching created vendors.

Staff Authorization Required

Vendor creation is restricted to DJB Employees only. You must first log in to the portal using your official employee credentials before proceeding with vendor registration.

Employee Login Guide →

Step 1: Navigating to Vendor Management Card

Access the Vendor Management module from the Core Services section of your employee dashboard.

1 Log in to the official Delhi Jal Board portal using your employee credentials (Username & Password, followed by 2FA OTP).
2 On the main Employee Dashboard page, scroll down to the Core Services workspace area.
3 Locate the Vendor Management card among the service modules.
4 Click on the Details button located at the bottom-right corner of the Vendor Management card.
Employee Privilege Note: Only authenticated DJB employees with authorized vendor management privileges can view and access the Vendor Management card options.

Step 2: Accessing New Vendor Registration

Selecting the New Vendor Registration workflow from the module options screen.

1 After clicking Details, the Vendor Management screen will load displaying all available action tiles.
2 Locate the card titled VENDOR_NEW_REGISTERATION (New Vendor Registration).
3 Click on VENDOR_NEW_REGISTERATION card to open the complete Vendor Registration form.
https://dev-djberp.nitcon.in/digit-ui/employee/vendor-management

Step 3: Filling New Vendor Details

Entering accurate agency background, contact information, and mandatory service type credentials.

MANDATORY NOTE:
The Service Type field MUST be selected as EKYC (or ekyc) for registering eKYC operational vendors.
Mandatory Vendor Form Fields:
1 Vendor Name *: Enter the full official business/company name of the vendor agency (e.g. CHECK Pvt. Ltd.).
2 Phone Number *: Enter the 10-digit primary contact mobile number for the vendor (+91 format).
3 Email: Enter the official corporate email address of the vendor organization.
4 Service Type *: Click the dropdown menu and select EKYC (Mandatory for eKYC operations).
5 Contract Start Date * & Contract End Date *: Select the active contract validity period dates using the date picker.
6 Zone *: Select the designated operational DJB Zone assigned to this vendor agency.
7 Owner Name *, Gender, Date of Birth *: Fill in the owner's official identification details.
https://dev-djberp.nitcon.in/digit-ui/employee/vendor/add-vendor

Step 4: Filling Property Address Form

Scrolling down to complete the physical location and regional address records of the vendor.

1 After filling the primary Vendor Details, scroll down the form page to reach the Property Address section.
2 Address Type *: Select the address category (e.g., Permanent or Registered Office).
3 City *: Select the operating city (e.g., Delhi).
4 Fill in additional locality details such as Pincode, Door/Building Number, Street/Gali Name, House No., Latitude, Longitude, Assembly, and Ward.
https://dev-djberp.nitcon.in/digit-ui/employee/vendor/add-vendor#property-address

Step 5: Reviewing & Submitting Vendor Details

Performing final verification of all entered information before submitting to the database.

1 Kindly review all entered information once carefully (Vendor Name, Phone, Service Type = EKYC, Contract Dates, and Address).
2 Ensure there are no missing mandatory fields marked with a red asterisk (*).
3 Click on the dark blue Submit button located at the bottom-right corner of the screen.
Validation Check: If any mandatory field is missing, the system will highlight the empty inputs in red before submission.

Step 6: Confirmation Message & Searching Created Vendors

Verifying successful vendor creation popup and locating your vendor in the searchable vendor directory.

1 If all details are valid, a green notification banner pops up displaying: "Vendor created successfully".
2 You will automatically get redirected to the Vendor Service Search / Vendor List page.
3 Searching for Created Vendors: On the Vendor List screen, ensure the Vendor tab is active.
4 Type the vendor name into the Vendor Name search field and click the blue Search button.
5 The data table will display the matching vendor record along with Vendor Name, Mobile Number, Creation Date, Service Type, and status toggle.
Vendor Creation Completed! The newly created vendor is now registered in the system and ready to perform eKYC surveys and application assignments.
