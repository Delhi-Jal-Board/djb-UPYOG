Supervisor Portal Login & 2FA Setup Guide

What this page helps with: This manual helps registered Supervisors authenticate into the DJB portal via Citizen Login, complete initial 2FA Mobile Authenticator setup and account profile activation, and reach the Supervisor Dashboard to manage field surveyors and eKYC operations.

Supervisor Registration Required

Supervisors must be registered by their respective Vendor agency prior to logging in. Ensure your vendor has generated your supervisor profile and provided your initial login password.

Supervisor Creation Guide →

Step 1: Navigating to Official Delhi Jal Board Portal

Accessing the primary Delhi Jal Board Integrated Enterprise Management System homepage.

1 Open your web browser and navigate to the official Delhi Jal Board portal URL:
2 The portal landing screen will open, featuring the main navigation header and the top-right blue LOGIN button.

Step 2: Accessing Citizen Login Dropdown

Opening the authentication menu and selecting Citizen Login for supervisor access.

1 Click on the blue LOGIN button located at the top-right corner of the portal header.
2 A dropdown menu will expand showing two authentication portals: Citizen Login and Employee Login.
3 Click on Citizen Login to proceed to the citizen services portal.

Step 3: Accessing eKYC Module from Citizen Dashboard

Selecting the eKYC service tile to trigger the Single Sign-On (SSO) login flow.

1 Upon selecting Citizen Login, you will land on the Citizen Services dashboard displaying operational modules.
2 Locate and click on the eKYC button tile visible in front on the services grid.

Step 4: DJB Single Sign-On (SSO) Authentication

Entering registered supervisor mobile credentials and vendor-assigned password.

Supervisor Credential Notice: Username is the supervisor's 10-digit mobile number registered during supervisor creation by the vendor.
1 You will be redirected to the DJB Single Sign-On (SSO) login form (`Welcome to Delhi Jal Board Access Portal`).
2 In the Username * field, enter your registered 10-digit supervisor mobile number.
3 In the Password * field, enter the temporary or permanent password provided by your vendor officials.
4 Click the blue Sign In button to authenticate.

Step 5: Mobile Authenticator Setup (First-Time Login 2FA)

Configuring Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for initial account activation.

First-Time Login Note: This 2FA setup screen appears only during your first login to bind your mobile authenticator app.
1 Download and install a supported Authenticator App on your smartphone (FreeOTP, Microsoft Authenticator, or Google Authenticator).
2 Google Authenticator Download Link:
Download Google Authenticator on Google Play Store
3 Open the installed authenticator application on your smartphone and select Scan a QR code.
4 Scan the QR Code displayed on the portal screen with your mobile camera.
5 After scanning, your app will display a 6-digit Time-based One-Time Password (TOTP) code for Delhi Jal Board SSO.
6 Enter the 6-digit code into the One-time code * field on the screen.
7 Optionally enter a name in the Device Name field (e.g., Supervisor Smartphone).
8 Click the blue Submit button to complete two-factor setup and activate your account.

Step 6: Update Account Information (First-Time Setup)

Completing mandatory profile information after mobile authenticator setup.

First-Time Login Note: The Update Account Information form occurs only during your first login to finalize your supervisor account profile details.
1 After clicking Submit on the Authenticator Setup page, you will be presented with the Update Account Information screen (`Welcome to Delhi Jal Board`).
2 Fill in all required fields accurately:
• Email *: Enter your valid email address.
• First name *: Enter your first name.
• Last name *: Enter your last name / surname.
3 Ensure all fields marked with a red asterisk (*) are populated to pass mandatory validation checks.
4 Review your profile details and click the blue Submit button.

Step 7: Supervisor Portal Dashboard (Successful Login)

Accessing your active supervisor session and operational portal modules.

Login Successful! You are now signed into the Delhi Jal Board Portal as a Supervisor. Your profile name and registered mobile number will appear in the top-right header.
1 Upon submitting your profile information, the system completes authentication and automatically redirects you to the Supervisor Portal Dashboard.
2 The top welcome banner displays a greeting with your supervisor profile name (e.g., Good Afternoon! Ekyc Supervisor Mannual).
3 From here, supervisors can access assigned services including eKYC, Vendor Management, My City, Water Tanker, Water & sewerage, Public Grievances, and Surveys.
