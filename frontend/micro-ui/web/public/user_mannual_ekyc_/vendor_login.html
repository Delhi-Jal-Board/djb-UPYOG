Vendor Portal Login & 2FA Setup Guide

What this page helps with: This manual assists onboarded Vendor agency personnel with accessing the DJB portal, authenticating via Citizen Login with their registered mobile number and DJB-issued password, configuring Mobile 2FA Authenticator (Google Authenticator) during first login, updating profile details, and accessing the Vendor management dashboard.

Vendor Account Requirement

Vendor accounts are pre-created by DJB Officials. Ensure your official registered mobile number and login credentials have been issued by DJB administrators before logging in.

Vendor Creation Guide →

Step 1: Navigating to Official DJB Portal

Access the official Delhi Jal Board Integrated Enterprise Management Portal homepage.

1 Open your web browser and navigate to the official Delhi Jal Board integrated portal URL: https://uat-ejalsewa.delhi.gov.in/digit-ui/home .
2 You will see the portal homepage featuring service search and quick links.
3 Locate the blue LOGIN button at the top-right corner of the header menu.
https://uat-ejalsewa.delhi.gov.in/digit-ui/home

Step 2: Selecting Citizen Login Option

Opening the top-right LOGIN dropdown menu and choosing Citizen Login.

1 Click or hover over the LOGIN button in the top-right header bar.
2 A dropdown menu will appear presenting two access options: Citizen Login and Employee Login.
3 Click on Citizen Login from the dropdown menu to proceed to the citizen/vendor portal.
https://uat-ejalsewa.delhi.gov.in/digit-ui/home#login-dropdown

Step 3: Accessing Login from Citizen Services Sidebar

Navigating into the Citizen Services dashboard and selecting the Login option.

1 After selecting Citizen Login, you will land on the Citizen Services page (https://uat-ejalsewa.delhi.gov.in/digit-ui/citizen).
2 On the left sidebar MENU, locate the Login option with the power/key icon.
3 Click on Login to open the Single Sign-On (SSO) authentication page.
https://uat-ejalsewa.delhi.gov.in/digit-ui/citizen

Step 4: Single Sign-On (SSO) Authentication

Entering your registered vendor mobile number as Username and official DJB password.

MANDATORY USERNAME NOTE:
For Vendors, the Username field MUST be the 10-digit registered mobile number provided during vendor creation by DJB officials.
1 In the Username * field, type your 10-digit Registered Mobile Number.
2 In the Password * field, type the official password assigned to your vendor account by DJB officials.
3 Click the blue Sign In button to authenticate.
https://uat-ejalsewa.delhi.gov.in/auth/realms/djb/protocol/openid-connect/auth

Step 5: Mobile Authenticator Setup (First-Time Login)

Activating account security by linking a smartphone Mobile Authenticator app.

First-Time Login Requirement: When signing in for the first time, the portal will display the Mobile Authenticator Setup page to pair your 2FA TOTP security key.
1 Download & Install Authenticator App: Install any supported authenticator app on your smartphone (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or FreeOTP).
📌 Google Authenticator Play Store Link: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.google.android.apps.authenticator2
2 Open the installed Authenticator application on your mobile phone.
3 Tap Add Account (+) or Scan a QR code in the app, and point your phone camera at the QR barcode shown on the screen.
4 Once scanned, your mobile app will display a 6-digit live One-Time Code (OTP) for Delhi Jal Board SSO.
5 Enter the 6-digit code into the One-time code * input field on your computer screen.
6 Click the blue Submit button to complete the 2FA setup and enter your vendor workspace dashboard.
Setup Complete! Your mobile authenticator is now registered. On future logins, simply enter the 6-digit code from your mobile authenticator app after entering your password.
https://uat-ejalsewa.delhi.gov.in/auth/realms/djb/login-actions/required-action

Step 6: Update Account Information (First-Time Login)

Completing mandatory profile information required only during initial vendor account activation.

First-Time Login Note: The Update Account Information screen occurs only for first-time login after authenticator setup is completed.
1 In the Email * field, type your official corporate email address.
2 In the First name * field, enter your first name or primary agency representative name.
3 In the Last name * field, enter your last name or agency designation.
4 Review all required fields and click the blue Submit button.
https://uat-ejalsewa.delhi.gov.in/auth/realms/djb/login-actions/first-login-update

Step 7: Successful Vendor Portal Login & Dashboard Access

Accessing your vendor homepage and managing core operational modules.

1 After clicking Submit on the Update Account Information screen, you will be successfully logged in to the vendor portal workspace.
2 The portal homepage displays a personalized greeting (e.g. "Good Evening! Vendor Manual") along with your active user profile in the top-right header.
3 Available Vendor Services & Modules: From the dashboard, vendors can access essential operational modules including:
• 📋 eKYC: Process consumer verification records and application entries.
• 🚰 Water Tanker: Track water supply requests and tanker distribution.
• 🤝 Vendor Management: Manage agency profile details and field surveyor assignments.
• 📢 Public Grievances: File and track resolution status of consumer complaints.
• 📁 Documents & Surveys: Review uploaded compliance files and active survey tasks.
Login Complete! You are now fully authenticated and ready to manage vendor operations on the Delhi Jal Board portal.
https://uat-ejalsewa.delhi.gov.in/digit-ui/citizen
